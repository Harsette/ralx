A few plain terms.

Draft, last updated 18 October 2026

These draft terms cover this website and the planned RalX Cloud service. The legal operator, governing law and final service terms need confirmation before publication. Contact hello@ralveto.com.

The open-source software

RalX’s public release is intended to be available under Apache-2.0. The license shipped with the release governs your use, copying and modification of that code. These website terms do not replace it. The release and license must be published before the installation links are ready for public use.

Your accounts and agents

Connect only accounts you are allowed to use. You choose your connected agents, their access and the tasks they run. Check the exact request before you approve it. An approval is not a guarantee that a third-party service will complete the task or that it can be undone.

Passwords and browser access

RalX keeps saved passwords out of agent tool replies and checks the site before filling. A signed-in browser still gives access to your account. A client with raw browser debugging access may inspect filled password fields. Do not give that access to an agent you do not trust. MFA, CAPTCHA, unusual login forms and provider restrictions can require your help.

Local, self-hosted and hosted

Local RalX runs on your computer. Self-hosting runs in the Cloudflare account you choose, using your own provider apps and keys. You are responsible for that setup and its provider costs. RalX Cloud is coming soon. Joining its launch list does not buy a service, reserve a price or promise a launch date.

Other services

Connected websites, Google, Cloudflare, WorkOS, agent apps and model providers have their own terms and data practices. RalX does not grant rights to those services or remove their account rules. Their availability and permissions can change.

Use it responsibly

Do not use this website or a hosted RalX service to access someone else’s accounts, bypass their approval, or disrupt the service. We may block abusive requests to protect the service.

No promise of a perfect run

Software and connected services can fail. Keep backups, review important actions and use only accounts and agents you trust. The open-source license describes the code’s warranty and liability terms. Final hosted service warranty and liability terms, subject to applicable law, remain to be approved.

Privacy and contact

Read the draft privacy policy for account data, Google API use and the launch email list. Questions can go to hello@ralveto.com. This draft does not establish a final governing law or legal operator.
