Privacy, in plain words.

Draft, last updated 18 October 2026

This draft explains RalX’s local software, independently self-hosted setups, and planned RalX Cloud. Hosted Cloud is not yet publicly available. The legal operator, retention schedules and production providers need confirmation before this policy is approved. Contact hello@ralveto.com with questions or deletion requests.

On your computer

Local RalX keeps saved credentials encrypted on your computer. It uses them for the tools and website logins you connect. Your agent receives task results, which can include private account content. RalX does not send a saved password to the agent as a tool reply. A browser client with raw debugging access may still inspect a populated password field. Software running as your user, or an administrator, can access local files and memory.

In your own cloud setup

When you self-host, the Cloudflare account operator controls the database and encryption key. Cloud source encrypts tokens and approval payloads. Account labels, granted scopes, activity metadata and summaries may also be stored. Local and cloud modes have different features. Read your host’s policy if someone else operates your setup.

Connecting Google

Connecting Google can supply your Google account identifier, email, granted permissions, and access and refresh tokens. When you choose a supported task, RalX can process Gmail message headers and bodies, Drive file metadata and content, and action results. Local YouTube tools may process channel or video information and user-requested uploads. Hosted cloud currently implements native Gmail and Drive, not native YouTube.

We use that access for the account features and agent tasks you choose. Listed risky actions, such as sending messages or moving items to Trash, require approval. Ordinary reads may run automatically. The Google consent screen shows the permissions requested for your connection.

Google’s Limited Use requirements

RalX handles information obtained through Google APIs under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use this information only for the account features and agent tasks you choose. We share the information needed for those tasks with the agents and services you authorize, as described in this policy. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Human access is limited to your explicit permission for specific data, necessary security investigations, legal obligations, or aggregated internal analysis permitted by Google’s policy.

This is a draft commitment, not a claim of Google verification. Before hosted launch, the operator must confirm compatible handling by connected agents and model providers.

Who receives task data

Your connected agents and their configured model or service providers receive the task results you request. Google and other connected services process requests sent to them. A cloud setup uses Cloudflare for hosting and storage, and WorkOS for sign-in. Their roles depend on how your host configures RalX. Google data does not necessarily stay inside RalX. Check the terms and data settings of the agents you connect.

The Cloud launch email list

If you join the list on this website, we store your email address and the time you joined in Cloudflare KV. We use it to tell you when RalX Cloud launches. We do not add it to an unrelated mailing list or sell it. Email delivery is not yet connected. To leave the list, email hello@ralveto.com.

The signup form uses a hidden spam field and a temporary hash of the connecting IP address and minute to slow repeat requests. That rate-limit entry expires after two minutes. It is not used for tracking across websites. The website does not set analytics cookies. Your browser contacts GitHub to display the repository’s star count; GitHub receives the usual network request information.

Keeping and deleting data

Local uninstall keeps vault data and logs so you can recover them. You control their deletion on your computer. A self-host operator controls its database and backups. Hosted retention for tokens, approvals, activity, logs, staged files and backups has not been set in this draft. The email list remains until launch or a deletion request; a final retention schedule must be confirmed before publication.

Removing a connection, revoking a provider grant, and deleting an account are different steps. Cloud connection removal deletes the saved connection but is not a promise to revoke the grant at Google or erase every activity record or backup. You can revoke Google access in your Google account. Revoking an agent blocks its next RalX request. It cannot recall a result already returned to an agent or remove that agent’s chat history.

Questions and changes

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact hello@ralveto.com. We may need to verify that the account or email belongs to you. This draft will change before approval to name the legal operator, confirm actual logging and backups, and set retention and deletion times.
